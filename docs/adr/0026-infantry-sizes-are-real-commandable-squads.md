# Infantry sizes are real commandable Squads

The Player's review after Issue 62 established that reducing only the number of representative figures did not solve army scale or handling. Infantry will support real, independently commandable Squad sizes: Carthaginian melee infantry uses 24/48; Rome uses 24/60/120 as the baseline, with 64 reserved as an alternative to compare against 60 during visual approval. Individual men remain unsimulated drawings as required by ADR-0004, with one Figure per surviving infantryman. Size is selected at recruitment; splitting and merging existing Squads are deferred. The starting recruitment-price hypothesis applies current resource costs to 24 men and scales proportionally, subject to balance validation. Payroll is proportional to surviving men, at type-specific rates. Billing/rounding and combat formulas remain under design; the chosen sizes do not imply historical authenticity for every roster entry.

This deliberately accepts more commandable objects and a balance revision in exchange for smaller tactical formations. Specialists have a single starting size per type: 24 slingers, 12 Numidian cavalry and one elephant Figure with separate abstract combat strength. Specialist prices and effectiveness require calibration alongside infantry. Combat, garrisons, assaults and blockades must account for actual force and troop type so that increasing the number of Squad entities does not automatically multiply military power.

The procedural art direction remains unchanged; individual figure dimensions may be reconsidered. Visible infantry losses leave persistent holes within the recruited formation, with stable survivor positions instead of continuous rear-rank centering. This is a visual treatment of aggregate losses, not individual-soldier simulation. Distributed group arrival positions make the increased number of small Squads manageable; temporary overlap through narrow passages remains permitted.
