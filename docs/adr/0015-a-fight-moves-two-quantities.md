# A fight moves two quantities, and a round of it is one simultaneous exchange

Combat is aggregate and **morale-primary**: two Squads in contact move the same two numbers on each other — how many men are left, and how close the Squad is to breaking — and a Squad that breaks runs rather than dying to the last man (ADR-0004). Every lever the plan names (the matchup matrix, flank and rear attacks, terrain, fatigue, ammunition, Payroll, being cut off) moves one of those two numbers and nothing else, which is what keeps a fight legible: a Player who has manoeuvred into an enemy's rear can predict that the enemy will be hit harder *and* waver faster, and a reviewer can read a rule that moves a third quantity as a rule that broke the model.

A round of contact is `EXCHANGE_TICKS` (5) ticks — half a second — and it is resolved **from the state as the round began**: every blow in a round is computed before any of them is applied, so it cannot matter which Squad the list walked first, and two Squads that hit each other in the same round both hit with the strength they had when it started. Nothing is drawn from the Match's random source. A fight is therefore a pure function of the world, a replay reproduces it exactly, and a balance change is measurable rather than sampled.

Every modifier is a **ratio of whole numbers** applied by multiplication then integer division, in a fixed order (files, matrix, where the blow lands, how well the striker is guarding itself, ground, fatigue, then the volley as plain men). Rounding once per step is not the same as rounding at the end, so the chain is written down and a test can predict a number by hand from it; the advantage ratio (`3/2`) and the disadvantage ratio (`2/3`) are exact reciprocals, so neither side of a matchup is quietly favoured by the integers.

**Considered Options**: a continuous drain on every tick — rejected because a morale shock of one point against a morale of ten would break a Squad in a second and leave every lever the plan lists without room to move it. A health bar with rout as a special case — rejected because the plan's whole design is that morale is the resource: a Squad at full strength and no nerve is a beaten Squad, and a model whose primary number is hit points cannot say so. Rolling a die per casualty for texture — rejected because it moves the generator's cursor, and a fight whose outcome depends on a draw is a fight a replay has to reproduce rather than understand. Two separate resolution passes, attacker first — rejected because it makes the order of the Squad list decide who strikes first.

**Consequences**: morale has two recovery paths, and only two. A routed Squad forms up by itself once the ground behind it is quiet, and a paid Squad recovers a point a Season through Payroll (`chargePayroll`); there is deliberately no in-the-field recovery, because recovering a point every few seconds would make a Season of Payroll worth nothing. Being cut off from Territory is read for now as "no settlement the Faction holds within `SUPPLY_RADIUS`", charged at the Season boundary beside Payroll — C6 replaces that inference with real Territory and per-Season Attrition, and `inSupply` is the one function that changes. The men a fight costs are **residue**, not state: they are recorded beside the Match in a `WeakMap`, drawn from the death frame the art already has, and swept after about thirty seconds, so corpses never enter the hash and a Match that recorded a thousand of them hashes as the Match that recorded none.
