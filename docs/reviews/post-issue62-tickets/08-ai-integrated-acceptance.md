# P8 — Visible AI pressure through the normal web Match and integrated acceptance

Published: [#71](https://github.com/AChiabodo/Hannibal-ad-Portas/issues/71). Parent: [#63](https://github.com/AChiabodo/Hannibal-ad-Portas/issues/63). Triage: ready-for-agent. P aliases refer to the adjacent published index.

## What to build

The normal web Match visibly encounters an opponent that contests the theatre within difficulty-appropriate active time. Diagnose/fix the actual remaining failure in the ordinary entry/host/pacing/rendering path and prove the revised economy, army management and learning experience together. Closing this ticket requires real effects and visible evidence, not merely an AI constructor or accepted Command log.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Commit a reproducible regression/diagnostic path capturing seed, human Faction, difficulty, active ticks, pause/speed settings, own permitted contact and meaningful pressure outcomes. Reproduce the user's symptom or a sharply defined absence-of-contact/pressure failure before attributing it to a guessed AI cause. Preserve evidence if the originally reported case cannot be reproduced.
- [ ] Trace ordinary menu/direct entry → configuration → real browser host → both Controllers → normal pacing/animation → permitted Observation → rendered enemy/contact Notice/camera navigation. Diagnose visibility/rendering/notice problems separately from recruitment/scouting/aggression. Manual host stepping alone cannot pass this path.
- [ ] Validate active-minute contact/pressure limits: Tiro ≤5/≤10; Centurio ≤3/≤6; Imperator ≤2/≤4. Use viable normal-start scenarios and documented passive/controlled-human policies, not extra enemy resources, a demo army, global vision or forced fabricated attacks.
- [ ] Use a predeclared fixed headless matrix of ten distinct seeds × both human Factions × all three difficulties for breadth. Require every sampled case to meet the goals or report a failure; do not drop inconvenient seeds or replace real pressure with repeated submitted Orders. Record the finite sampling limitation.
- [ ] Contact appears in permitted human sight and is demonstrated as actual on-screen troops with a contact Notice and working camera access. Pressure has a concrete rule-backed contention/combat effect/attempt, with evidence of progress or an actual refusal, rather than just an issued Move/Blockade.
- [ ] Fix any remaining AI/host integration defect under the new costs, sizes, military evaluation and group movement; keep current no-cheating difficulty semantics and aggregate-army rules. Demonstrate sustainable openings for both Factions and retain deterministic Replay of headless cases.
- [ ] Run a bounded representative browser matrix using normal pacing, covering both human Factions and all difficulties across the evidence set. Observe a normal-interface playthrough for each Faction, including real input, visible contact and pressure. Supported faster pacing is allowed but active time and wall time are recorded separately; no debug advance bypass is substituted for this evidence.
- [ ] Demonstrate one cohesive entry-to-conflict experience: configure, understand/build the economy, recruit small/large or asymmetric forces, command a group, see losses/holes, encounter pressure, consult reference and return/Resume. Feature tickets keep their own checks; this final scenario verifies integration rather than replacing them.
- [ ] Run complete required acceptance after integration, plus relevant explicit visual evidence. Recheck focused ≤60-second and smoke ≤90-second budgets and report environment/results. Summarize each evidence source and every unrun gate; do not call headless success visual acceptance or SwiftShader hardware 60 fps.
- [ ] Record the changed rules/content baseline and bounded outcome/balance limitations for #29, full-density performance responsibility for #31 and release responsibility for #32. Do not close or modify those existing issues merely because this ticket passes.

## Testing decisions

Breadth belongs at the public Match/Controller/Observation seam; a few ordinary paced browser scenarios verify the actual web path. Structured diagnostics may retain internal test-only explanations, but the human-facing rendering/notices never reveal unseen enemy decisions. This ticket may use early read-only baseline evidence; final closure waits for the integrated product and all specified evidence.

## Shared constraints

Preserve existing procedural art direction/pipeline, Italian UI, seeded/integer rules, filtered Observation and no-cheating AI. Never weaken contact/pressure assertions to match current behavior. Timing targets are active simulation time, not wall-clock time or unbounded guarantees against arbitrary human counterplay.
