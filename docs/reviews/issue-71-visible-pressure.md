The first command plays the predeclared seeds **1–10**, both human Factions and
Tiro/Centurio/Imperator: exactly 60 cases, with no replacement or exclusion.
The optional seed runs all six configurations on that seed. Failure exits 1;
invalid CLI input exits 2. The human is passive: no Commands, ordinary seeded
start, no extra men/resources/vision. Limits in active minutes remain 5/10,
3/6 and 2/4 for contact/pressure. This finite sample is no universal guarantee
and gives no guarantee against arbitrary human counterplay.

Each JSON line records configuration, pause/speed, active ticks/milliseconds,
first permitted live enemy with coordinates, first contact Notice, first actual
wall damage or an enemy physically occupying a Blockade ring, final/replayed
hashes and maximum unpaid Seasons. An issued Move/Blockade is insufficient.
Blockade occupation is a rule-backed contention attempt, **not** a claim that
the next seasonal starvation has occurred. `computeMilliseconds` measures elapsed
headless execution time, not CPU usage or normal paced browser wall time. Diagnostic opponent snapshots
are explicitly headless-only and never supplied to human rendering/notices.
The log is regenerated by the same seed/configuration on this build and replayed
with the AI disabled. A failed timing/replay/payroll/Notice check is reported as
failure, never filtered out.

## Reproduced failure and corrections

`issue-71/baseline.jsonl` was captured before policy changes: **10/60 passed**.
Seed 1, passive Carthage human, Centurio/Imperator: no live contact or pressure
by the bound. Rome built a villa first, consuming opening construction stocks,
and then a farm; no payable army emerged in that window. This is an economic
opening failure independently of rendering. Against a Rome human on seed 1,
contact could occur without timely pressure; accepted AI orders were not proof.

The opening now prioritizes a first affordable detachment and its recurring
income before a Tier upgrade. A staffed market under construction contributes
projected income only if its remaining work finishes **before** the next Payroll
boundary; current population, labour, costs, food floors and treasury reserves
still gate recruitment. Scouting first crosses the theatre toward its opposite
home-relative region, using only own home, dimensions and observed terrain;
after that region is observed, ordinary frontier exploration resumes. This
does not disclose where the opponent actually starts, and wrong-way searches
remain possible. No difficulty resource/vision/force rule changes were made.

Separately, the Notice journal lacked an enemy-sighting event. A first live
enemy now produces Italian `Nemico avvistato`, deduplicated per enemy per Match.
Its camera target is the **observed tile**, retained as history; it never follows
an unseen enemy. Ordinary own-Squad/settlement navigation retains its existing
resolution behavior. Enemy sprites still come from permitted `visible.enemies`.
